# Findings — `product-intent.md`

## 1. Verdict

**PASS-WITH-MAJORS.** The register is complete against the brief's product items (1–11, 14, 15, 17, 18), all 54 IDs are unique and every ID used anywhere in `docs/discovery/` resolves to the register (grep over all ten documents + evidence: zero orphan IDs), the PoC acceptance criteria are mirrored verbatim by `minimal-poc-plan.md` §3–5, no ATM noun re-enters as architecture, and the glossary ban list is honoured except for two soft drifts ("sessions", "reviewed role evolution"). One MAJOR blocks a clean pass: §1.1's three factual claims about ATM (single harness exercised; confinement and autonomy spikes failed; other harnesses and surfaces never reached) were added in v3.1 from evidence but carry no `[ev:atm-salvage#Fn]` citation, and one of them over-attributes (the U2 autonomy spike is recorded as a model-compliance failure "not a mechanical-isolation failure" [ev:atm-salvage#F24], and a Telegram binding *was* materialised in spike S2 [ev:atm-salvage#F3]). The remaining findings are consistency and ambiguity items: the HarnessSelectionPolicy layer list differs across glossary / HB-03 / downstream docs, AD-01 vs the overlay model is readable two ways, §2's persistent column omits the User Overlay, the brief's discovery mandates (items 12, 13, 16, 19) are not restated although the document claims to restate the brief faithfully, and several dependents still quote the pre-v3.1 wording of §1.1 and HB-03.
